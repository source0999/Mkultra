## Defining Kabbalah: "Revealed" vs. "Concealed"

The word **Kabbalah** literally means "tradition" or "that which is received." In an objective historical context, it refers to the esoteric and occult side of Jewish thought.

To explain these terms:
* **Esoteric**: Knowledge intended for only a small "inner circle."
* **Occult**: Means "hidden." It refers to the study of hidden forces—such as the influence of angels, demons, or the power of divine names.

## Nigleh vs. Nistar

Judaism is one of the world's oldest esoteric traditions. Kabbalists treat the Hebrew Bible as a cryptic, coded text operating on two levels:

* **Nigleh (The Revealed)**: The literal stories and laws accessible to everyone.
* **Nistar (The Concealed)**: The hidden, mystical meanings regarding the nature of God and the universe.
