## 1. Revealed and Concealed

<Slide>
  <div>
    ### Tradition as a “Received” Text
    The word Kabbalah is often translated as “tradition” (that which is received). In an objective historical framing, it points to the esoteric side of Jewish thought: meanings intended for a small circle rather than the general public.

    Esoteric here means “inner,” while occult here means “hidden forces or spiritual machinery” that the average person cannot directly see.
  </div>

<Slide>
  <div>
    ### Nigleh and Nistar (Two Levels)
    Kabbalists treat the Hebrew Bible as a coded text that operates on two layers:
    - <b>Nigleh</b> (The Revealed): the literal stories and laws accessible to everyone.
    - <b>Nistar</b> (The Concealed): hidden mystical meanings about the nature of God and the universe.

    The “inner” dimension was historically protected through language barriers and by protective rules about who should learn what.
  </div>
