1. Defining Kabbalah: "Revealed" vs. "Concealed"

The word Kabbalah literally means "tradition" or "that which is received"[cite: 3]. In an objective historical context, it refers to the esoteric and occult side of Jewish thought[cite: 4].

To explain these terms plainly:

  • /Esoteric: Refers to knowledge intended for only a small, specialized "inner circle"[cite: 5].
  • /Occult: Simply means "hidden." It refers to the study of forces that the average person cannot see, such as the influence of angels or the power of divine names[cite: 7].

“Kabbalah” to “Cabal”

According to Etymonline, the English word Cabal originally referred to the mystical interpretation of scripture[cite: 14, 15]. By the 1600s, because this information was restricted to the elite, the definition shifted to describe an intriguing society meeting privately[cite: 16, 17].
